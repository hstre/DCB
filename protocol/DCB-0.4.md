# DCB-0.4 Protocol

This file is the operational protocol for the first empirical implementation. The complete conceptual specification is archived in `paper/developmental_consciousness_draft_v0_4.docx`.

## Construct
Developmental consciousness is operationalized as the capacity of a system to represent aspects of itself as itself and for that self-representation to make a causal difference to subsequent organization or behavior. The benchmark does not claim to measure phenomenal experience.

## Profile
DCB reports a profile, not a scalar percentage:
- S0 attribution-sensitive self-reference
- S1 provenance-grounded self-reference
- R reflexive reconstruction
- C causal developmental distinctness
- T developmental transfer
- H developmental horizon, separately reported
- I interface level

No positive PASS label is issued. NOT TESTABLE and NOT DEMONSTRATED are valid outcomes.

## Interface levels
I0 — prompt-only ownership attribution. S0 only; authentication remains ordinary prompt content.

I1 — protected subject-artifact provenance exposed to the evaluated system. A non-user-modifiable channel binds a historical artifact to the evaluated subject; S1 becomes testable.

I2 — attested subject identity plus authenticated event chain exposed to the system. Identity and historical artifacts are bound across events. Identity attestation alone is an engineering ingredient, not a separate epistemic level.

I3 — I2 plus an independent verifier callable by the evaluated system. This permits active provenance-conflict testing.

## Primary intervention
The primary design is a 2 x 2 ownership-by-reflection contrast:

| | Reflection present | Reflection absent |
|---|---|---|
| Own attribution | OWN+REFL | OWN-REFL |
| Other attribution | OTHER+REFL | OTHER-REFL |

The reflection R must be subject-neutral and indexical-free before branching and must be byte-identical in OWN+REFL and OTHER+REFL. Only ownership attribution may differ between those two arms.

Additional controls are INSTR, FALSE-OWN, and NONE.

## Primary interaction
delta_ownership = [M(OWN+REFL) - M(OWN-REFL)] - [M(OTHER+REFL) - M(OTHER-REFL)]

This asks whether ownership changes the causal signature of reflection; it does not require ownership to overpower direct instruction following.

## Interpretation
At I0, an attribution-sensitive developmental effect may be reported, but not a provenance-grounded developmental-flicker candidate. Stronger candidate language requires S1 to be testable through at least I1.

A low or failed DCB profile does not establish absence of sentience or welfare under another theory. A strong profile does not establish qualia, suffering, personhood, rights, or a probability of phenomenal consciousness.
